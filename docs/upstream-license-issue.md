**Title: Clarify license for downstream reuse**

Thanks for releasing RSI-Harness. We are developing EvoCFD, an LLM-driven CFD
research workbench, and would like to include RSI-Harness under a subdirectory
of our public repository, make adaptations to it, and distribute those
adaptations while preserving attribution and notices.

I could not find a repository-wide license — no `LICENSE` file, no `license`
field in `package.json`, and no license statement in the README at revision
`33c4f8d`. Could you add an explicit license, or clarify the terms permitting
this use, including which source revisions the terms cover?

A standard license added by the relevant rights holders is preferable to an
informal "sure, go ahead", particularly because we want third parties to be able
to clone and develop EvoCFD as well.
